# Incident #002 — Evidence

This folder contains supporting evidence for the missing drive letter troubleshooting incident.

## Evidence Files

### missing-drive.png

Screenshot showing the `IT-LAB-DISK` volume detected by Windows Disk Management without an assigned drive letter.

### drive-restored.png

Screenshot showing the successful restoration and verification of the `E:` drive.

## Troubleshooting Evidence

The evidence demonstrates:

1. The storage volume was detected by Windows.
2. The volume was healthy and formatted as NTFS.
3. No drive letter was assigned to the volume.
4. The drive letter was restored using DiskPart.
5. The volume was successfully accessed and verified after the fix.
